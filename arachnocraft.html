This Privacy Policy explains how Zeenith Base LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play the mobile game ARACHNOCRAFT : Spider Hero (the "Game") on Android devices. By using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers for the Game and we do not receive or store your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (world changes, inventory, missions, level, coins, unlocked suits, settings) is saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

  • Delete local data: uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps.

5. Permissions

The Game uses the Internet and network state permissions to load ads, and the Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) used by the ad partners. The Game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data on our own servers, we do not retain it. Data collected by ad partners is retained according to their own policies. Data transmitted by the ad partners is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, or to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for advertising. Because we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights directly with the ad partners through the links above, or contact us and we will help.
