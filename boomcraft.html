BOOMCRAFT : Blast World (package: com.zeenith.boomcraft)

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeenith Base LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play BOOMCRAFT : Blast World (the "Game") on Android. By using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. We do not have servers that receive your personal information.

Game data stored on your device: your progress, inventory, world changes, missions and settings are saved locally on your device only. We cannot access this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game is free and supported by advertising. To show ads, the Game uses third-party advertising and mediation services. These services may automatically collect and process the following information:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, screen size
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • App information: app version and how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completion)
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs

This information is used to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and, where you have consented, show personalized ads. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Rewarded ads

Some buttons in the Game (for example Free Nuke, Full HP or Revive) let you choose to watch a video ad in exchange for an in-game reward. Watching these ads is always optional. The Game can be played without them.

4. Your consent and choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized. You can accept or refuse personalized ads. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

5. Your rights (US residents, including California)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, delete or opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information. You can limit ad tracking at any time using the options below, or contact us for help.

6. How to limit ad tracking

  • On Android, open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Uninstall the Game to stop all data collection by the Game and its third-party services.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their own privacy policies. We use reasonable measures to protect the Game, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International transfers

Third-party services may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. These providers apply their own safeguards as described in their privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
