MontCraft: Verity Horror – Privacy Policy

Developer: Zeenith Base LLC · Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game MontCraft: Verity Horror (package com.zeenith.montmont), published by Zeenith Base LLC ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on Android.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information directly.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, XP, selected skin, difficulty and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The game is free and supported by advertising. Ads are delivered through the following third-party services, which may collect and process information from your device:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (ad mediation) – privacy policy
  • Unity Ads – privacy policy
  • Google AdMob – privacy policy · how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Meta Audience Network – privacy policy

These providers may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, device model and operating system, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, language, app interactions with ads, and crash or diagnostic data related to ads. They use it to show ads, measure ad performance, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized.

Some ads are optional "rewarded" videos that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. Watching them is never required to play.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are located in these regions, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy option in the game menu.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Stopping all collection: uninstalling the game stops all data collection by the game and its SDKs.

4. Children

MontCraft: Verity Horror is a horror game intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and information was collected, contact us and we will help you take appropriate action.

5. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information processed by the advertising partners listed above is retained according to their own policies. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying reports from them (for example, number of ad impressions and revenue).

6. Security

We rely on the security measures of the platforms and partners listed above. Communication between the game and ad services uses encrypted connections.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold personal information about you directly, most requests are best directed to the advertising partners above. You can also contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the game after an update means the updated policy applies.
