Privacy Policy – PUNCHCRAFT : One Hit Crafting

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeenith Base LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play the mobile game PUNCHCRAFT : One Hit Crafting (the "Game"), available on Google Play under the package name com.zeenith.punchcraft.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (levels, stars, tokens, hero level, skins, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes this progress.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and is supported by advertising. We use the Unity LevelPlay mediation platform, which shows ads from the following partners:

  • ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies)
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC)
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms, Inc.)

To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, these partners may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • device model, operating system version, language and screen settings
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region)
  • app and ad interaction data (for example, ads shown, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data related to ads

This information may be used to show personalized ads where the law allows it. The partners process it under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy

Rewarded ads are always optional. The Game can be completed without watching any rewarded ad.

3. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the Game.
  • You can stop all data collection by the Game by uninstalling it.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will work with our partners to delete it.

5. Data security and retention

We do not operate servers that store your personal information. Data collected by our advertising partners is kept and protected according to their own policies linked above.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit its use for advertising. Because we do not hold your personal information ourselves, most requests are handled by our advertising partners through the links above; we will gladly help you with any request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
